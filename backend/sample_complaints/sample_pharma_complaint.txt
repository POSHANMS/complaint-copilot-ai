PHARMACEUTICAL PRODUCT COMPLAINT REPORT

Date of Complaint: 2024-07-15
Complaint Source: Retail Pharmacy
Customer Name: MedCare Pharmacy, Mumbai

Product Details:
Product Name: Atorvastatin Tablets
Strength/Grade: 20 mg
Batch/Lot Number: ATR-2024-B0421
Manufacturing Date: 2024-01-10
Expiry Date: 2026-01-09

Complaint Type: Contamination
Quantity Affected: 3 blister packs (90 tablets)

Detailed Description:
The pharmacist at MedCare Pharmacy, Mumbai, reported that customers returned
three blister packs of Atorvastatin 20mg Tablets (Batch ATR-2024-B0421)
due to the presence of unusual black discoloration on approximately 30% of
the tablets. The tablets also had an abnormal odour described as chemical
or musty.

Upon further inspection, two of the three packs also showed signs of seal
integrity failure -- the blister foil was partially peeled on one edge,
potentially exposing tablets to ambient moisture. Customers reported that
one patient experienced gastrointestinal discomfort after consuming a tablet
from this batch, though no hospitalisation occurred.

The pharmacy has quarantined the remaining stock from this batch (approximately
12 additional blister packs) pending investigation by the manufacturer.
